# Adventurer’s Tome 2.1.12

Tome’s **Compact scenes** and **Scenes** controls are available to GMs. Players use Foundry’s standard navigation to open scenes they can access.

Update through **Foundry → Add-on Modules** or use the [stable manifest](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GuteboysFactory/AdventurersTome/main/channels/gold/module.json).
