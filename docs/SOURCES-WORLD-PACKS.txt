3 November 2026

The opt-in Tolkien pack 1.1.0 contains 44 locations and one people/faction
reference (Dúnedain). It is a curated starting collection, not a complete
Tolkien encyclopedia. No maps, artwork, biographies or encyclopedia articles
are bundled. It does not describe the events or history of this campaign.

Labels, selected aliases and short descriptions come from Wikidata structured
data, retrieved 2–3 November 2026. Each record carries its Wikidata item URL,
CC0 license and retrieval date. Descriptions are structured-data descriptions,
not copied article passages. Local subtype labels classify places and people
into Tome's location/faction categories; a people is not an individual NPC.

Source snapshots and the reproducible build script are retained in the development
tests/fixtures directory. Ambiguous labels such as the Chetwood family name were
not imported as places. The place uses its distinct verified Wikidata identity.
Unattested spellings are not silently installed as permanent aliases.

Tolkien Gateway remains a saved future reference. No content from that site
has been imported. This is an independent reference aid, not an official Tolkien
product. CC0 applies to Wikidata's structured data.

The setting defaults to no pack and follows the Foundry world. Selecting Tolkien
loads the local file only. Ordinary analysis never calls an external lookup or AI
service. A unique typed reference can create a missing private World entry under
Tome's decision policy; a reference never fabricates a Foundry UUID. Campaign
identities, permissions, paused entries and explicit GM decisions are checked
before a write. Library information is stored with its provenance and remains
separate from session-derived campaign facts. Disabling the library stops its
analysis assistance and does not delete campaign documents already created.
