# Adventurer’s Tome 2.1.13

- **Dice table.** Dice roll across the map, bounce off visible tokens, and appear in chat as they settle. Players can choose dice materials, colors, D6 models and markings, sounds, and a fade of 5 to 15 seconds. The GM can choose immediate results for the world.
- **Numeric pools.** Use the chat dice button to mix D2, D4, D6, D8, D10, D12, D20, and D100 in compatible systems.
- **Genesys VTT.** Build a pool by clicking the six colored dice types. Tome animates the symbols on the actual rolled faces for public results. Genesys resolves the roll and keeps its own result card.
- **Daggerheart.** Hope and Fear use distinct gold and purple dice. Daggerheart handles critical successes and other outcomes.
- **Realm Guard.** Realm Guard keeps its D6 pool. Compatible Realm Guard builds can send the base roll, new Wise or Fate dice and Versus rolls to Tome for animation.

The Foundry test baseline is 13.351.

Update through **Foundry → Add-on Modules** or use the [stable manifest](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GuteboysFactory/AdventurersTome/main/channels/gold/module.json).
