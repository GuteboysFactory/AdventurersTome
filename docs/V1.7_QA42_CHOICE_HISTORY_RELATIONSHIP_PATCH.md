# qa.42 — GM identity history and explicit relationship coverage

This is a local Foundry test patch on the existing qa.42 branch. It does not change the module version, publish a release, or introduce the planned language-pack/index engines.

## Root causes

1. Source-specific GM selections reached entity creation, but the earlier source identity / prose Memory stage did not consult those selections. A previously selected name could therefore retain ambiguous or unresolved Memory evidence.
2. Choices from another Session/Quest were not consulted as historical identity evidence. Older `linkExisting` records also use the legacy decisions store rather than the newer source choices store.
3. The English relationship provider did not cover cooperation (`works with`), residence (`staying at`), treatment (`treated`), role appositions, explicit two-person continuing friendship, or a safely bound organization reference such as `the order`. Source excerpts in Facts do not themselves constitute canonical relationship evidence.

## Identity behavior

`campaignReviewLearning.historicalChoiceFor` reads existing GM selections without creating global aliases. It collapses proven projections to authority UUIDs and accepts a historical mention only when its selections agree on one authority. Independent exact names or registered aliases block reuse, including namesakes in additional root collections. Conflicting selections and deleted targets remain REVIEW; they cannot create replacement identities.

`atSsiResolveMention` gives the explicit decision in this source priority, then retains the existing source canonical / inline reference rules, then consults conservative historical evidence. Stable current-source references continue to take precedence over historical interpretation. The source decision API remains compatible with legacy callers.

`resolveCandidates`, `atMeScopedResolution`, `atMeFromDiscoveryRow`, and record normalization carry the selected authority and private `identityChoice` provenance through discovery, Memory, persistence, and reanalysis. The resulting mention remains attributed to its own current source; historical choice sources describe why its identity was resolved, not where the narrative statement occurred.

GM choice metadata remains private. No identity is merged merely because its display name resembles another name; unregistered variants and genuine conflicts still require Review.

## Relationship behavior

The common relationship registry adds WORKS_WITH (symmetric cooperation), STAYS_AT (inverse Hosts), and TREATED (inverse Treated by). These are separate from employment and membership.

The existing English provider recognizes bounded role appositions and attached predicates. A unique appositive subject in an introductory/reporting sentence owns its relationship rather than the narrator. An organization reference requires one safe organization antecedent. `The two ... friends` requires exactly two resolved people in that sentence. Multiple possible antecedents are not selected automatically.

The ordinary canonical evidence path still supplies inverse relationships, source spans, provenance, polarity, certainty, temporal scope, permissions, and stable IDs. Treatment in the past remains Historical, current cooperation/residence/friendship remains Current, denied membership remains negative, and hedged residence remains Possible. Unsupported uncertain narrative statements do not become assertions of guilt or invented relationships.

These additions are generic English rules, not name-specific conditions. Full Swedish discovery/relationship support remains separate work; UUID resolution and explicit GM identity choices do not depend on English prose.

New tests cover real prose Memory after source choices, historical reuse across sources and root types, legacy choices, conflict isolation, deleted targets, player privacy, independent aliases, generic quest/reload semantics, and the reported healer/merchant narratives as both native and Contact identities.
